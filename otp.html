Encrypt


    
    

    
The pad is the key. Generated once, shown once, stored nowhere. Exchange it over a secret channel and destroy every copy after one use. Any reuse of pad bytes, in any part, breaks perfect secrecy.

Self-test

    

    

    
SHA-256 known answers; inflate against fixed- and dynamic-Huffman streams encoded by an independent zlib; deflate round-trips on text, binary, repetitive and random data; container round-trips for both key modes; wrong-key, truncation and bit-flip rejection; the pigeonhole check that random ciphertext does not compress.
